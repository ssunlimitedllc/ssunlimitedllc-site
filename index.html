About

SS Unlimited, LLC is a Maryland-based network service provider serving businesses, government teams, and MSPs. We handle the design, deployment, monitoring, and automation of the network and infrastructure your organization runs on — as an ongoing partner or on well-defined projects.

Our team brings 12+ years of hands-on experience across healthcare, government, defense, and cybersecurity training environments. We currently engineer nationwide network infrastructure for SANS Institute, deploying and managing 50+ MikroTik routers and Ruckus wireless systems for live cybersecurity training events serving 2,000+ students and maintaining 99.5%+ uptime across venues.

Beyond that, we've stood up and supported enterprise environments across healthcare and non-profits — managing Active Directory and Microsoft 365 for hundreds of users, running SCCM patch programs across hundreds of endpoints, deploying and monitoring firewalls, VPNs, and virtualization platforms. If your organization needs infrastructure that keeps a real business running, that's what we do.

Whether you need a partner watching your network every day or a defined project delivered on a fixed schedule — get in touch.
